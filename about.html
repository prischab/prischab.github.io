About me

Hi, I'm Prischa. I'm in my final year of B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at MIT World Peace University, Pune.

I grew up in Muscat, Oman, and lived there until I finished school. Moving to Pune for university was a big change, and I think living in two places taught me to adjust quickly and stay curious about how things work.

What I enjoy most is taking an idea all the way to something people can actually use. In college, that led to two research papers: one on a face recognition attendance system I built on a Raspberry Pi, and one on predicting household energy use with LSTMs.

Right now I'm interning at Choice TechLab, where I've worked on things I never expected to touch as a student: a tool that listens to customer calls in Hindi and English, a voice cloning service for Indian languages, the backend for a company productivity app, and a simulation that clears traffic for ambulances in Pune.

I like writing about what I build, because explaining something simply is the best test of whether I really understand it. I'm graduating in 2027 and looking for roles in AI/ML, data, or software.
